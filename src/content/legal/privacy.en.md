# Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

Glosc AI ("we", "us", or "our") respects and protects the privacy of our users. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you visit gloscai.com and our related products.

## 1. Information We Collect

- **Access information**: browser type, device information, IP address, access time, and other standard log data used for site operation and security troubleshooting.
- **Information you provide**: such as your name and contact details when you reach out to us via email or a form.
- **Third-party service information**: this site displays open-source project information using the public GitHub API, without transmitting your personal information to GitHub; the friendly-links footer retrieves a link list from a third-party endpoint purely for display purposes.

## 2. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

- Maintain and improve the functionality and experience of our site and products;
- Respond to your inquiries and feedback;
- Protect the security of our site and user accounts;
- Comply with applicable laws and regulations.

## 3. Cookies and Local Storage

This site uses your browser's `localStorage` to remember your theme (light/dark) and language preference. This data is stored only on your device and is never uploaded to our servers.

## 4. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share necessary information only when:

- We have your explicit consent;
- Required to comply with applicable laws, court orders, or requests from government authorities;
- Necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of Glosc AI, our users, or the public.

## 5. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 6. Children's Privacy

Our products are designed for adult users and we do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If you believe we may have collected information from a minor, please contact us using the details below so we can address it promptly.

## 7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page along with an updated "Last updated" date. Please review it periodically.

## 8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out via our [GitHub organization](https://github.com/Glosc).
